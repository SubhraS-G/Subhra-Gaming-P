# Portfolio Structure

## 01 — HERO

SUBHRA GAMING
Gamer • Streamer • Content Creator

Portrait + SUBHRA background typography.

## 02 — WHAT I PLAY

Show the games that genuinely represent the current channel.

Example:
- VALORANT
- GTA V
- Minecraft
- Free Fire
- BGMI

Only keep games that are actually part of the current content strategy.

## 03 — FEATURED CONTENT

Show:
- Best YouTube videos
- Strong livestreams
- Shorts/Reels

Use real thumbnails and direct links.

## 04 — CONTENT SHOWCASE

Show creative execution without repositioning the user as a designer:

- Thumbnail showcase
- Short-form edits
- Motion moments
- Stream visuals
- Gaming campaign concepts

## 05 — CREATOR STATS

Use current verified numbers only.

Examples:
- YouTube subscribers
- Monthly views
- Average views
- Instagram reach
- Engagement rate

Always show the measurement period.

## 06 — SETUP

Gaming setup / hardware / streaming equipment.

This can naturally support hardware-brand collaborations.

## 07 — COLLABORATE

Gaming brands
Gaming peripherals
Hardware
Apparel
Games
Launch campaigns
Livestream integrations
Product integrations

## 08 — MEDIA KIT

Provide a downloadable PDF.

## 09 — CONTACT

Professional email + social links.
