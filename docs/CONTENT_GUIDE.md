# Content Guide

## Featured Content

Choose quality over quantity.

Recommended starting set:
- 3 strongest YouTube videos
- 2 strongest livestreams
- 3 strongest Shorts/Reels
- 6–9 strongest thumbnails

## Thumbnail Showcase

Do not call this "Graphic Design Services."

Call it:
- Thumbnail Showcase
- Content Visuals
- Thumbnail Archive

The purpose is to demonstrate the quality of the creator's content.

## Motion Showcase

Do not lead with "Motion Designer."

Use:
- Content in Motion
- Motion Showcase
- Gaming Visuals

Include:
- Stream intro
- Product integration
- Gaming montage
- Animated thumbnail
- Social campaign concept

## Spec Work

Unofficial brand concepts can demonstrate capability before paid collaborations.

Always label them clearly:

`CONCEPT / SPEC WORK — NOT COMMISSIONED BY THE BRAND`

Potential categories:
- Gaming mouse
- Keyboard
- Headset
- Gaming laptop
- Apparel
- Energy/beverage brand

## Brand Safety

Never imply an unpaid concept was an actual collaboration.
Never display a brand logo as a client unless there was a real relationship.
