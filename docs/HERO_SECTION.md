# Hero Section Specification

## Composition

### Background
- Near-black base
- Very subtle radial lighting
- Soft vignette
- Fine grain/noise
- Minimal HUD geometry

### Typography

Large background word:

`SUBHRA`

Properties:
- Very large
- Behind portrait
- Low contrast
- Grey/metallic feel
- Slight transparency
- Partially hidden by the portrait

### Portrait

The user's real face should be the focal point.

Recommended source:
- High-resolution portrait
- Chest/waist-up framing
- Clean background or easy cutout
- Natural facial appearance
- No heavy AI stylization

### Identity

Primary:
`GAMER • STREAMER`

Secondary:
`Gaming. Streaming. Creating.`

Optional longer copy:
`A gamer at heart, a streamer by choice, and a creator through everything I do.`

### Information Modules

Use small UI panels for:

- CURRENTLY PLAYING
- CONTENT
- STATUS

Example:

CURRENTLY PLAYING
VALORANT

CONTENT
LIVE • GAMEPLAY • SHORTS

STATUS
ONLINE

### CTAs

Primary:
`WATCH CONTENT →`

Secondary:
`COLLABORATE →`

### Socials

- YouTube
- Instagram
- Discord/community

## Animation

Entrance sequence:
1. Background fades in.
2. `SUBHRA` reveals.
3. Portrait rises/fades in.
4. UI modules appear.
5. Identity text appears.
6. CTA becomes active.

Interaction:
- Very subtle mouse parallax.
- Portrait moves slightly more than background.
- Background word moves slightly less.
- UI moves slightly more.
