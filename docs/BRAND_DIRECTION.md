# Brand Direction

## Core Identity

# SUBHRA GAMING

**Gamer • Streamer • Content Creator**

The portfolio must communicate the following hierarchy:

1. Gamer
2. Streamer
3. Content Creator
4. Design/editing/animation as supporting creative capabilities

Never reverse this hierarchy.

## Brand Statement

The site should make a visitor understand within a few seconds:

> Subhra is a gaming creator/streamer who creates gaming content and is open to gaming-related brand collaborations.

## Visual Personality

- Dark
- Premium
- Cinematic
- Gaming-inspired
- Minimal
- Technical/HUD-inspired
- Personal rather than corporate

## Avoid

- Agency/creative-studio positioning
- "Hire me as a designer" messaging
- Generic design portfolio terminology
- Excessive neon
- Excessive glassmorphism
- Overly complicated 3D effects
- Too many animations
- Generic esports-team aesthetics

## Supporting Skills

Design, editing and animation should appear as evidence of content quality:

- Strong thumbnails
- Motion graphics
- Product integrations
- Stream visuals
- Short-form edits
- Gaming campaign concepts

They should not become the headline identity.
